Gusheng (Justin) Pan

Information Engineering · University of Cambridge

I'm a third-year undergraduate in Information Engineering at the University of Cambridge. My research is on LLM reasoning and evaluation: how models reason, what knowledge did they apply, where that reasoning breaks down, and how to measure it in ways that reflect real capability rather than familiarity with the test.

Most recently I was a research assistant at Oxford's OATML with Dr Yihong Chen, where I built AxiomShift, a benchmark that alters axioms to separate reasoning apart from recall. I'm always glad to hear from people working on reasoning, evaluation or knowledge in language models!

Publications

AxiomShift: Altering Axioms to Evaluate LLM Reasoning at Knowledge Boundaries

Gusheng Pan, Yihong Chen

NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on Mathematical Reasoning and AI (MATH-AI) · Poster

A 700-question benchmark across seven mathematical and scientific worlds, each with one axiom changed, tested across 26 models. As model capabilities increase, falling back to training-world priors makes up a growing fraction of total failures.

PDF OpenReview Code

Memorized or Memo-rized: Prior Leakage of Procedural Knowledge in Large Language Models

Kexin Wang, Lin Li, Yi Ren, Zihuiwen Ye, Gusheng Pan, Yug D. Oswal, Yarin Gal, Yihong Chen

NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on Agents in the Wild (AIWILD) · Poster

Swapping or renaming SQL and Pandas operators in the supplied documentation shows that models across 17 open-source LLMs stay anchored to their prior, even when told to follow the documentation.
